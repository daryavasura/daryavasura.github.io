Process

How a teardown works

  1. BenchmarkI put your app next to 5 to 10 competitors.
  2. FindI find where users drop and what is unclear.
  3. DecideYou get 5 to 10 changes ordered by impact, each with a reason.
  4. MeasureWe choose the metrics before launch, so the result is visible.

I lay every screen out as one flow, add notes, and mark where the user is asked for something before they have seen any value.

I compare icon options on a real home screen, in light and dark, and mark the one I recommend, with reasons.

Explorio app

Imaguru Startup Hub accelerator, 2026. Non-technical co-founder: strategy, product, marketing and customer research. Engineering by my co-founder.

Hypothesis
People need accurate local data that Google Maps cannot give. They will verify places themselves, so the map stays useful for them and for others.
Test
About 20 customer interviews, competitor research, unit economics, monetization forecasts, and an MVP tested by 13 users. The goal was not only many verifiers but returning ones: a place's green "freshly checked" status expired after a month, so people had to keep coming back.
Result
We learned that people like using verified data and it sparks their interest and enthusiasm, but they are not willing to verify places themselves. So the data could not stay fresh, and we found no way to change that. We stopped the project.
